About

On the Vieux-Quai Since 2016

A Neighbourhood Restaurant.

Élise Bourque grew up in a New Brunswick fishing village, between her grandmother's kitchen and the wharf where her father unloaded the catch. After ten years in the kitchens of Montreal and Halifax, she came home to the region in 2015 with one wish, a table of her own, open every day.

Her cooking borrows from French bistros, the Italian tables of Montreal and the Maritime recipes she grew up on. She likes generous plates, sauces that simmer for hours and vegetables roasted until they caramelize. She spends every service in the kitchen, then steps into the dining room before dessert to greet the tables.
