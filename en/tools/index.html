Tool demos

How it works

The site you're browsing is ready to launch as it stands, with every tool it already has. It can also go further, with features that put the site to work for the restaurant. Those take technical skills and more complex data, and OyengaMédia, Nokaba's technical partner, can design them and build them in. Here are seven examples, shown as demos only.

Included with the site

Tools already built in

They ship with the site and need no custom development.

  • Table booking

    Guests pick a date and party size, and the request goes straight to the restaurant.

    See it in action

  • Quote requests

    Groups, private events and catering go through one form that spells out the occasion.

    See it in action

  • Contact form

    Every message arrives tagged with its subject, and “Other” opens a free-text field.

    See it in action

  • Interactive map

    The map of the Vieux-Quai loads once cookies are accepted.

01Reservations

Live table booking

Guests see the tables that are genuinely free for their date and party size, and book without picking up the phone. The booking lands straight on the host stand’s floor plan.

Who does what

  • The guestpicks a day, a party size and an open time.
  • The systemholds the table and sends the confirmation.
  • The hostwatches the evening fill up on the floor plan.
  • The remindergoes out by text the day before.

What the restaurant provides

  • The floor plan and the size of each table.
  • Opening hours and how long a sitting lasts.
  • Cancellation rules and the guarantee for large tables.

Designed and built by OyengaMédia on request.

02Takeout

Online ordering

Guests build their order, pick a pickup time and pay online. The kitchen gets the ticket on its tablet and guests follow their order right up to the counter.

Who does what

  • The guestorders, picks a time and pays.
  • The kitchengets the ticket and has it ready on time.
  • The systemtells the guest when the order is ready.
  • Stripetakes the payment.

What the restaurant provides

  • The takeout menu, with prices.
  • A tablet in the kitchen or at the counter.
  • A Stripe account.

Designed and built by OyengaMédia on request.

03Busy nights

Text-message waitlist

On full nights, guests join the list from their phone and go for a stroll on the wharf. A text lets them know the moment their table is ready.

Who does what

  • The guestjoins the list and tracks their place.
  • The hostflags the table that frees up.
  • The systempicks the party that fits the table and texts them.
  • The guestconfirms they're on their way.

What the restaurant provides

  • A text messaging service, billed per use.
  • A tablet at the host stand.
  • Table sizes, for matching parties.

Designed and built by OyengaMédia on request.

04Shop

Online gift cards

Guests give a dinner out in a few clicks. The card goes out by email or text with its code, and the balance can be checked online at any time.

Who does what

  • The buyerpicks an amount and writes a note.
  • The systemcreates the code and sends the card.
  • The servertakes the meal off the balance at the bill.
  • Stripetakes the payment.

What the restaurant provides

  • The amounts on offer and the card design.
  • A Stripe account.
  • Your card validity rules.

Designed and built by OyengaMédia on request.

05Catering and events

Quotes and deposits online

Clients follow their event from first request to the big day. The quote is approved online, the deposit is paid in one click and every step arrives by text.

Who does what

  • The clientapproves the quote and pays the deposit.
  • Nadia, eventsprepares the quote and confirms the menu.
  • The kitchensees the week's events and their quantities.
  • The systemsends the updates and keeps a record of the file.

What the restaurant provides

  • Group menus and their prices.
  • The deposit percentage and cancellation rules.
  • A Stripe account.

Designed and built by OyengaMédia on request.

06Regulars

Loyalty program

Every dinner and every order adds points to a regular’s card. A dessert, a discount or dinner for two unlocks along the way.

Who does what

  • The regularcollects points and redeems rewards.
  • The serveradds the points at the bill.
  • The systemtracks the tiers and creates the codes.
  • The ownersees who comes back, and how often.

What the restaurant provides

  • The rewards and their tiers.
  • A link to the point-of-sale system, to add points.
  • Hosting in Canada.

Designed and built by OyengaMédia on request.

07Notifications

Text reminders and reviews

Booking reminders go out on their own the day before, and guests confirm with one word. After the meal, a text invites them to leave a review online.

Who does what

  • The systemsends reminders and review requests.
  • The guestconfirms or cancels with one word.
  • The hostsees confirmations on the floor plan.
  • The ownerreads the reviews and replies.

What the restaurant provides

  • A text messaging service, billed per use.
  • Guest consent, collected at booking.
  • The link to your online reviews page.

Designed and built by OyengaMédia on request.
