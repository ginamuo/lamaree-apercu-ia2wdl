The Story of La Marée

Ten Years of Cooking in Belhavre.

Élise Bourque opened La Marée in the spring of 2016, in the old hardware store on the Vieux-Quai. She kept the original beams and tall windows, then filled the room with blond-wood chairs and long shelves under the pendant lights.

Ten years on, she still slips out of the kitchen to serve the first lunch plates herself. Everything is made here, from pasta rolled at dawn to the day's desserts, with vegetables from Ferme des Hauts-Vents and fish from the wharf across the way.

Years of Home Cooking

Why La Marée

What Makes La Marée

Everything at La Marée is cooked on site and served at lunch and dinner, Tuesday to Sunday, to big tables and tables for two alike. Here are three reasons to come in on a Tuesday night with nothing to celebrate.

Made Here

Pasta, stocks, brunch breads and desserts are made here every morning, never from a freezer bag. Our chef buys from farms nearby.

Lunch and Dinner

The dining room opens Tuesday to Sunday, lunch and dinner, with Sunday brunch from 10 a.m. Walk-ins are welcome at the bar.

Room for Groups

Four spaces can be booked for 12 to 90 guests, with a group menu and a quote within 48 hours. Nadia looks after every event.
