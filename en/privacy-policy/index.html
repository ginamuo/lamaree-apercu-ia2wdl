Privacy

What La Marée collects when you book or write to us, and what we do with it.

Who is responsible

La Marée restaurant, 42 rue du Vieux-Quai, Belhavre, New Brunswick E4X 1A2, is responsible for the personal information collected on this site. We protect it under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), which applies to private businesses in New Brunswick.

Nadia Fraser, our dining room manager, looks after this policy. If you have a question, write to her through the contact form or call 506 555-0142.

Contact us

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to give us. A reservation asks for your first name, email, phone number, the date and the size of your party. A quote request or a message also includes your subject and what you wrote.

If you tell us about an allergy or a special diet, we add it to your reservation so the kitchen can adjust your plate.

No payment is ever taken on this site. Deposits for group events are paid at the restaurant or over the phone.

How we use it

We use this information to confirm your reservation and to reach you if something changes. We never sell it or share it for advertising.

We keep reservation history for twelve months, then delete it. Allergy notes are deleted after your visit, unless you ask us to keep them for next time.

We won't send you promotional offers without your consent.

Cookies and third parties

The site sets the cookies it needs to work. With your consent, given in the cookie banner, it also counts visits anonymously. It never tracks your browsing for advertising.

The map on the site uses OpenStreetMap tiles. Your browser requests those tiles directly from OpenStreetMap, which receives your IP address. The video on the home page is hosted on our own site and relies on no third-party service.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see your information or to have it deleted. We reply within thirty days, as the law requires.

If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
